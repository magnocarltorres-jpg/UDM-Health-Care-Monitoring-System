Privacy Notice

UDM Health Care Monitoring System

Draft — For Institutional Review Only Universidad De Manila's Data Protection Officer or authorized legal reviewer must confirm the lawful basis, official privacy contact, retention schedule, and processor and transfer details before publication.

Draft prepared November 3, 2026. This notice describes the system based on its current implementation and information supplied by its operator. It is not legal advice.

2. Information the System Handles

Patient and Student Information

  • Student number and patient ID
  • Full name, course, and year level
  • Age and sex
  • Residential address
  • Guardian's name, relationship, and contact number

Health and Clinic Information

  • Height and weight
  • Blood pressure and body temperature
  • Heart rate and oxygen saturation
  • Body Mass Index (BMI)
  • Reason for visit and assessment notes
  • Drug-test dates, results, and remarks
  • Medicine-dispensation records

Staff Account Information

  • Staff ID, name, and assigned role
  • Email address, when provided
  • Profile photo and account status
  • Password hash and password-reset request details

Contact Form Information

  • Name and email address
  • Subject and message

Information may be provided directly by patients, students, guardians, staff, or individuals submitting contact forms. Clinic personnel may also enter patient information when providing clinic services.

3. Purposes and Legal Basis

The system is used for the following purposes:

  • Registering and identifying clinic patients.
  • Maintaining patient profiles and clinic records.
  • Recording health assessments and clinic visits.
  • Documenting drug-test results and medicine dispensations.
  • Managing authorized staff accounts.
  • Processing password-reset requests.
  • Receiving and responding to contact inquiries.

Legal Basis: The applicable lawful basis for each processing activity, particularly sensitive personal information, has not yet been formally documented. The institution's Data Protection Officer must determine and approve the appropriate legal bases before publication.

4. Who Can Access or Process Information?

Access is restricted according to staff roles:

  • Administrators: Manage staff accounts and password-reset requests.
  • Nurses: Access shared clinic records through the authenticated backend.
  • Doctors: Access shared clinic records through the authenticated backend.

The current system does not assign patients to individual Nurses or Doctors. Authorized clinic personnel therefore access shared clinic records according to their assigned permissions.

External Service Providers

  • Supabase: Provides database and storage services. The configured project region was identified as Tokyo, Japan and must be verified.
  • Mailjet: Used to send staff account-related emails.
  • Resend: Used to deliver contact-form inquiries to the configured recipient.

The institution must verify the service providers, applicable agreements, processing locations, and safeguards for international data transfers.

5. Data Storage and Retention

Patient records and other application information are stored in the system's hosted database.

The browser stores a bearer authentication token in per-tab session storage for an eight-hour session, so logging out in one tab does not clear another tab's session. Clinic assessment history is retrieved from the authenticated backend and is not intentionally stored in browser local storage. The application removes its legacy local assessment-history cache when it loads.

Data Retention

The official retention schedule has not yet been supplied. The institution must establish retention periods for:

  • Patient and health records
  • Staff accounts
  • Password-reset requests
  • Contact inquiries
  • Database backups
  • Security and activity logs

Information should only be retained for approved periods and handled according to institutional policies and applicable privacy requirements.

6. Information Security

The system uses technical and administrative safeguards to help protect personal information, including:

  • Authenticated staff login.
  • Account activation and role-based access checks.
  • Backend authorization for clinic records.
  • Password hashing.
  • Database Row Level Security (RLS).
  • Server-side handling of sensitive database credentials.

The database has RLS enabled on its application tables, and direct table privileges have been revoked from public and client roles. The application accesses records through its authenticated backend API.

The backend uses a Supabase Service Role Key, which bypasses RLS. This credential must remain confidential and must never be exposed in frontend code or public repositories.

These security measures help reduce risks but cannot guarantee absolute protection against every possible threat.

Important: Patients and staff should not send medical records, health assessment details, or other sensitive personal information through the public contact form.

7. Your Privacy Rights

Individuals may exercise their privacy rights under applicable Philippine data privacy laws, subject to legal requirements and exceptions.

  • Right to be informed.
  • Right to access personal information.
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Right to object to certain processing activities.
  • Right to request erasure or blocking when applicable.
  • Right to data portability when applicable.
  • Right to file a complaint with the appropriate authority.

Privacy requests should be directed to the officially designated UDM Data Protection Officer or privacy office. The institution must provide its approved request procedure and official contact details before publication.

8. Children and Guardians

The system may process records of students below 18 years old and may collect guardian information.

The institution must establish appropriate procedures for processing minors' personal and sensitive personal information, including applicable requirements involving parents or legal guardians.

These procedures must be reviewed and approved by the institution's Data Protection Officer before implementation.

9. Updates to This Privacy Notice

This Privacy Notice may be updated when the system's features, information-processing activities, or institutional privacy requirements change.

The latest approved version should be made available through the UDM Health Care Monitoring System.
